# Function

1. Hold the wand and **right-click** an <ref item="minecraft:anvil"/> (or its variants) to "suck" it into the wand and carry it around
2. **Right-click** an empty spot to place the carried anvil into the world
3. While carrying an anvil, the wand glows and its tooltip shows the carried anvil

<tip>
Anvils moved with the wand can quickly set up temporary automated lines together with anvil-drop contraptions
</tip>

# Other

1. The absorbed anvil is kept as-is (its type / damage state are preserved)
2. One wand can only carry a single anvil at a time
