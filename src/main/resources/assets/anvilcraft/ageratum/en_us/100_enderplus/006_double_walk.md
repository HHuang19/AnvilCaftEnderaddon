# Blink

- Applies to **leggings**
- While enchanted, press the **blink key** (default **V**, rebindable in Controls) to **dash 5 blocks** forward
- Cannot pass through walls
- Max level: 3
- Each level reduces the cooldown; levels above 3 have no effect

# Notes

1. The dash distance is configurable (`doubleWalkDistance`, default 5)
2. After use it goes on cooldown; the remaining cooldown is shown on your screen
3. It cannot trigger while you are taking damage (hurtTime)
