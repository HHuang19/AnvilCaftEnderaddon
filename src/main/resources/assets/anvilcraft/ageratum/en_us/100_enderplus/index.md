# AnvilCraft: Ender+

Welcome to the content page of *AnvilCraft: Ender+*!

* Adds the **Magnet Wand** to carry and place anvils on the go
* Adds the **Power Block** and **Ender Pole** for easy power generation and cross-dimension transmission
* Adds the **Ender Talisman Pillar** and **Ender Amulet** to pocket your charms and take them across dimensions
* Adds the **Double Jump** and **Double Walk** enchantments for better mobility
