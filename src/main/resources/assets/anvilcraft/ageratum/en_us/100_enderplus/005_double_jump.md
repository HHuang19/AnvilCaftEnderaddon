# Double Jump

- Applies to **boots**
- While enchanted, press **jump** again in mid-air to perform a **second jump**
- Max level: 2
- Each level adds one jump segment; levels above 2 have no effect

# Notes

1. It triggers only while in the air, not flying and not swimming
2. The number of jumps refreshes after each landing
