# Hanging Charms

The Ender Talisman Pillar is a **2-block-tall** block used to display and store AnvilCraft charms.

1. Hold a **charm** and **right-click** the pillar to hang it on the side you are facing
2. Each side holds up to **4** charms; the four sides are independent
3. **Right-click** the pillar with an empty hand to take down the charm under your crosshair
4. Breaking the pillar drops all charms hung on it
5. The <ref item="anvilcraft_enderplus:ender_amulet"/> can be bound to the pillar. Once bound,
the effects of the charms hung on the pillar are transferred to the player carrying that amulet.
