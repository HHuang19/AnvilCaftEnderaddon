# Hanging Charms

The Ender Talisman Pillar is a **2-block-tall** multi-part block used to display and store AnvilCraft charms.

1. Hold a **charm** (an item with the charm data component) and **right-click** the pillar to hang it on the side facing you
2. Each side holds up to **4** charms; the four sides are independent
3. **Right-click** the pillar with an empty hand to take down the charm under your crosshair
4. Breaking the pillar drops all charms hung on it

# Working with the Ender Amulet

The <ref item="anvilcraft_enderplus:ender_amulet"/> can be bound to the pillar. Once bound,
the effects of the charms hung on the pillar are "transferred" to the player carrying that amulet.

# Other

- Emits light level 15
- The hanging side is decided by the direction you face when placing it
